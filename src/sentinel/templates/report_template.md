# Sentinel Safety Evidence Packet

## Context of use

Vigilo ranks public drug-event reporting patterns in FDA FAERS and assembles an evidence packet for a pharmacist or safety reviewer. It does not diagnose a patient, decide causality, or produce a regulatory submission. A person accepts, rejects, or escalates every finding.
